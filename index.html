📍 Local levels

Your device time zone supplies a regional estimate. Device location is only requested if you press the optional button below.

Correct the estimate below, especially when travelling or near the equator. New settings affect the next board or attempt, never a board already in play.

Weather uses a representative regional area unless you choose a closer approximate area. It may differ from your town.

Device location is optional. Coordinates are rounded to 1° cells (~111 km north/south) before being sent or saved. No exact GPS coordinates are retained. You can also enter an approximate area.
